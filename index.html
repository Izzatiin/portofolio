About me

I'm a product designer drawn to problems that are equal parts human and technical. Growing up in a busy Indonesian household taught me to notice small things: who gets left out of the group chat, which button my grandmother is afraid to press. I bring that same attention to how software should feel.

I'm finishing my degree in Visual Communication Design and recently interned as a product designer at a fintech startup in Jakarta. I'm open to full-time roles and excited for what's next!
