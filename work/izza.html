TL;DR

IZZA_ is a personal AI that lives inside your phone. The AI models run on the device itself, so it works without internet and your data stays on your phone. It's free, private, and offline.

It's the newest step in a series of local AI projects I've built: first on the web, then as an app for laptops, and now on phones.

Features

One app, many missions

Inside the app, the Mission Station (Stasiun Misi) gathers everything IZZA_ can do.

  • Chat about anything

    Write code, find ideas, translate, and summarize text.

  • Web search with sources

    Answers from Brave Search and GitHub, with every source listed.

  • Ask for a game, play it

    Describe a game and IZZA_ builds it in HTML and JavaScript. Tap Run to play.

  • Smart email

    Flags scam emails and marks verified senders as safe.

  • Sky map

    See the constellations and planets above you, and zoom in to Saturn.

  • Garden & space fishing

    100 fishing rods and 1,000 creatures, all controlled with natural language.

  • Endless collection

    A merchant shop and an astronaut wardrobe full of things to collect.

  • Pick your model

    Gemma 4, Qwen 3, Llama 3.2, Gemma 3n, DeepSeek R1, or find your own.

Under the hood

Your phone, your model

IZZA_ can run models like Gemma-4-E2B on the phone's GPU (about 2.6 GB), handling text, images, and voice. Besides chat, the app includes voice chat, voice transcription, asking about images, agent skills, a command lab, phone actions, and photo cutouts.
