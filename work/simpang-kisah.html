TL;DR

Simpang Kisah is a short film about Nopal, a university student going through heartbreak after his girlfriend, Dinda, leaves him. It explores the dynamics of a relationship through conversation.

I was fully responsible for post-production: editing the film from the first cut to the final version so the story reaches the audience with the right emotion.

My part

From the first cut to the final version

The whole post-production stage was mine. My goal was simple: make sure the story flows and every scene carries the right feeling.

  1. 01

    Building the story

    Putting the footage together into a first cut that follows Nopal's story from beginning to end.

  2. 02

    Shaping the emotion

    Adjusting the pace of each scene so the heartbreak and the conversations land with the audience.

  3. 03

    Finishing the film

    Polishing the final version in CapCut and Adobe Premiere, ready for release on YouTube.
