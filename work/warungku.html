Koperasi Warga

Closing Time, Counted

  • Shipped
  • Service design
  • Small business
  • Mobile
TL;DR

Owners of small family-run shops (warung) wanted to know one thing at closing time: did I earn more than I spent today? We designed Warungku, a bookkeeping app that answers that question without feeling like accounting.

I ran the field research and designed the core flows for logging sales and tracking customer debts.

Background

Every street has a warung

Warung sell everything from instant noodles to phone credit, usually run by one family from the front of their house. The cooperative we partnered with supports these shops, and kept hearing the same question from owners.

Problem

Finance apps were built for accountants

Most owners tracked sales in a paper notebook, or not at all. Debts from regular customers (kasbon) were written on scraps of paper and often forgotten, quietly eating into thin margins.

Owners did not want reports. They wanted one answer at closing time.

Research

Sitting behind the counter

We visited shops at different times of day and watched how owners handled sales, restocking, and debts. Owners usually had only one free hand, customer debts were the most stressful thing to track, and many families shared a single phone.

Process

Designing around two moments

We focused on logging a sale while serving a customer, and checking the day's result at closing time.

  1. 01

    Co-design with owners

    We sketched with owners at their counters and let them rearrange paper screens until the order made sense to them.

  2. 02

    Two-tap sales

    Frequent items appear as large buttons. Prices use the same short format owners already write in their notebooks.

  3. 03

    Debts with a name and a face

    Each debt shows the customer's name and photo. The app drafts a polite WhatsApp reminder the owner can send when they choose.

Testing

Fast enough to use while serving

Owners tried the prototype during real business hours. They could log a sale without putting anything down, and several kept using the debt list after the test ended.

Final product

One answer at closing time

The closing screen shows today's money in and money out in plain words, with open debts listed underneath.

Impact

Handed to the cooperative

Our designs and research notes became the starting point for the cooperative's own app.

Reflections

Remove before adding

Designing for people with little experience using apps taught me that every label we cut made the app easier to trust.
